# Japan main-talk evidence

Retrieved 23 July 2026. Only the claims below may feed `MAIN_TALK_EVIDENCE`. Company releases are labelled as company-reported or demonstration scope; simulations remain simulations.

## Japan primary-energy self-sufficiency

- Primary source: Agency for Natural Resources and Energy, [Energy White Paper](https://www.enecho.meti.go.jp/en/category/brochures/whitepaper/).
- Publication / reporting period: 2024 white-paper edition; FY2023 energy balance.
- Reference: `Energy White Paper · FY2023`.
- Approved slide wording: **Japan’s FY2023 primary-energy self-sufficiency rate was 15.3%.**
- Do not say: “15.3% electricity self-sufficiency”; do not derive an import-cost, reserve, oil-route, or LNG-route percentage from this value.

## Hormuz oil / LNG separation

- Approved evidence: none in the evidence module until each fuel has an exact dated primary-source URL and reporting period.
- Do not say: **“97% LNG via Hormuz.”** Oil and LNG are separate flows; an oil-dependency percentage cannot be reused as an LNG-transit percentage.
- Also excluded: the keynote’s household-cost and price-impact claims unless they receive their own exact dated sources.

## JEPX 2021 price range

- Primary source: Japan Electric Power Exchange, [spot-market trading data](https://www.jepx.jp/en/electricpower/market-data/spot/).
- Data period: late December 2020 to mid-January 2021.
- Reference: `Spot-market trading data · late Dec 2020–mid-Jan 2021`.
- Approved slide wording: **Day-ahead spot prices moved from below 10 to 251 JPY/kWh during the displayed late-December-to-mid-January window.**
- Do not say: the series alone proves manipulation or a single cause; always render the chart’s date window beside the values.

## Kyushu output control

- Primary source: Kyushu Electric Power Transmission and Distribution, [renewable-output control history](https://www.kyuden.co.jp/td_power_usages/out_ctrl_history.html), FY2025 Kyushu-mainland workbook.
- Data interval: 4 May 2025, 12:00–12:30.
- Reference: `Output-control history · 4 May 2025 · 12:00–12:30`.
- Approved slide wording: **Kyushu T&D recorded up to 5.09 GW of renewable-output control during the interval.** The workbook value is `509 × 10,000 kW`.
- Do not say: “5.09 GWh,” “energy wasted,” or an annual curtailment total. This is maximum power control, not curtailed energy.
- Excluded records: the working `1.76 TWh` FY2023 national total and `5.9 GW` April 2023 example are not in `MAIN_TALK_EVIDENCE` because this deck does not hold their exact primary bulletins.

## Shizen V2H demonstration

- Primary source: Shizen Energy / Shizen Connect, [“Shizen Connect completes one of Japan’s largest VPP demonstrations with EV charging and discharging”](https://www.shizenenergy.net/en/2024/02/16/sc_ev_vpp_demo_complete24/).
- Publication date: 16 February 2024; demonstration period: January 2024.
- Reference: `Press release · 16 Feb 2024 · Jan 2024 demonstration`.
- Approved slide wording: **In January 2024, Shizen Connect remotely controlled 186 household EVs through V2H equipment in METI’s FY2023 DER Aggregation Demonstration Project.**
- Qualifier: the release reports 90% control accuracy for economic DR and balancing-market control; display this only as **company-reported 90% control accuracy**.
- Do not say: the result applies to batteries, heat pumps, a named region, a commercial fleet, or delivered grid capacity; do not omit the company-reported qualifier.

## Kansai / Shizen HEMS demonstration

- Primary source: Shizen Energy / Shizen Connect, [“Shizen Connect and Kansai Electric Power conduct low-voltage VPP demonstration for capacity market”](https://www.shizenenergy.net/en/2024/05/30/sc_capacitymarket_lv_vpp/).
- Publication date: 30 May 2024; stated demonstration period: January–February 2025.
- Reference: `Press release · 30 May 2024 · demo scheduled Jan–Feb 2025`.
- Approved slide wording: **Kansai Electric Power and Shizen Connect planned a demonstration of HEMS-controlled residential storage batteries for simulated capacity-market demand response and technical-feasibility evaluation.**
- Scope: Kansai Electric Power controls residential batteries through its HEMS; Shizen Connect creates the simulated DR, plans control programmes, sends instructions to the HEMS server, and evaluates technical feasibility.
- Do not say: participant count, battery kW/kWh, delivered market value, commercial outcome, or proven capacity-market performance. Label the case **demonstration scope**.
