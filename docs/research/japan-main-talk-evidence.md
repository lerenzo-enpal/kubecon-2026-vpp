# Japan main-talk evidence

Retrieved 22 July 2026. This log intentionally uses conservative wording: the deck must not imply that an illustrative dispatch or a generic asset type is a Shizen Connect outcome.

## Japan energy and LNG dependency

- Primary source: Agency for Natural Resources and Energy, [Energy White Paper](https://www.enecho.meti.go.jp/en/category/brochures/whitepaper/).
- Approved wording: Japan’s FY2023 primary-energy self-sufficiency rate was 15.3%.
- Caveat: this is primary energy, not electricity; reserve, LNG-route, and household-cost statements require their own dated source.

## JEPX 2021

- Primary source: Japan Electric Power Exchange, [spot-market data](https://www.jepx.jp/en/electricpower/market-data/spot/).
- Approved wording: JEPX publishes the spot-market series for the January 2021 disruption.
- Caveat: retain the date window alongside any plotted price values.

## Kyushu curtailment

- Primary source: Kyushu Electric Power Transmission and Distribution, [official site](https://www.kyuden.co.jp/td_english/).
- Approved wording: renewable-output curtailment is an operational grid condition.
- Caveat: do not use an annual TWh total unless its exact period and original bulletin are linked.

## Shizen Connect

- Primary source: Shizen Energy, [Shizen Connect](https://www.shizenenergy.net/en/business/shizen-connect/).
- Approved wording: Shizen Connect is a VPP platform for distributed energy resources in Japan.
- Excluded claims: device count, MW, regional scope, heat-pump role, and market role unless explicitly stated on a linked programme page.

## ERAB / OCCTO

- Primary source: Agency for Natural Resources and Energy, [electricity measures](https://www.enecho.meti.go.jp/en/category/electricity_and_gas/electricity_measures/).
- Approved wording: ERAB provides policy and market context for resource aggregation.
- Caveat: avoid presenting ERAB as a universal licence or adding telemetry obligations without the programme rulebook.
